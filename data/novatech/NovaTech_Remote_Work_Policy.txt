NovaTech Solutions - Remote Work Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose
This policy defines the guidelines for remote and hybrid work arrangements at NovaTech Solutions.

2. Eligibility
- Employees who have completed 3 months of probation are eligible to apply for remote work.
- Approval depends on the nature of the role and manager’s recommendation.

3. Work Arrangements
- Hybrid Model: Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week.
- Fully Remote: Available only for specific roles with prior approval from HR and Department Head.

4. Working Hours
- Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST.
- Employees must be available on official communication channels during core hours.

5. Expectations
- Employees must maintain productivity equivalent to office work.
- A dedicated and quiet workspace is required.
- Video must be turned on during important meetings unless there is a valid reason.

6. Equipment
- The company will provide a laptop if required.
- Employees are responsible for maintaining a stable internet connection.

7. Review
Remote work arrangements will be reviewed every 6 months.
