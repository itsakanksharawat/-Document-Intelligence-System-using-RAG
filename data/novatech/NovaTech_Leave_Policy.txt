NovaTech Solutions - Leave Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose
This policy outlines the types of leave available to employees of NovaTech Solutions and the procedures for requesting leave.

2. Types of Leave
2.1 Annual Leave (Paid Time Off)
- Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Leave is accrued monthly at the rate of 1.5 days per month.
- A maximum of 5 unused days can be carried forward to the next year.
- Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance.

2.2 Sick Leave
- Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
- For absences of more than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate is required.
- Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

2.3 Casual Leave
- Employees can take up to 6 days of casual leave per year for personal reasons.
- Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave without prior approval.

2.4 Maternity Leave
- Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
- The leave can start up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

2.5 Paternity Leave
- Male employees are entitled to 7 days of paid paternity leave.

3. Leave Application Process
- All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal or email to the reporting manager and HR.
- The manager must approve or reject the request within 48 hours.

4. Unauthorized Absence
Absence without prior approval or proper documentation will be treated as unpaid leave and may lead to disciplinary action.
