NovaTech Solutions - Employee Onboarding Guide
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Welcome
Welcome to NovaTech Solutions. This guide will help you understand the onboarding process.

2. First Day
- Report to the HR desk at 9:30 AM.
- You will receive your employee ID, laptop, and access credentials.
- A buddy will be assigned to help you for the first two weeks.

3. Documents Required
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Bank account details
- Educational certificates
- Passport size photographs

4. Orientation
- Day 1: HR orientation and company overview
- Day 2: Department introduction and tool training
- Week 1: Completion of mandatory compliance training

5. Probation Period
- All new employees are on probation for 3 months.
- Confirmation will be based on performance and feedback.

6. Support
For any onboarding related queries, contact hr@novatech.com.
