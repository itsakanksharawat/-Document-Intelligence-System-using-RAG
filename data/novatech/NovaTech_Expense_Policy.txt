NovaTech Solutions - Expense Reimbursement Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose
This policy explains the rules for claiming official expenses incurred by employees.

2. Eligible Expenses
- Travel (local and outstation)
- Client meeting expenses
- Internet reimbursement for remote employees (up to ₹1,000 per month)
- Official software or tools (with prior approval)

3. Non-Eligible Expenses
- Personal meals
- Personal travel
- Entertainment without prior approval
- Alcohol

4. Claim Process
- All claims must be submitted within 15 days of the expense.
- Original bills or digital invoices are mandatory.
- Claims must be submitted through the expense portal or email to finance@novatech.com.

5. Approval
- Claims below ₹5,000 can be approved by the reporting manager.
- Claims above ₹5,000 require additional approval from the Finance Manager.

6. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims will be reimbursed within 10 working days.
