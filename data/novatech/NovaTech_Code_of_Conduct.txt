NovaTech Solutions - Code of Conduct
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose
This Code of Conduct sets the standards of behavior expected from all employees of NovaTech Solutions.

2. Professional Behavior
- Employees must treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and dignity.
- Discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, race, or disability is strictly prohibited.
- Harassment of any kind (verbal, physical, or online) will not be tolerated.

3. Integrity and Honesty
- Employees must act with honesty in all professional dealings.
- Misrepresentation of data, falsifying records, or providing false information is a serious offense.

4. Confidentiality
- Employees must not share confidential company information, client data, or internal documents with unauthorized persons.
- This obligation continues even after leaving the company.

5. Conflict of Interest
- Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company interests.
- Any potential conflict must be reported to the manager or HR immediately.

6. Use of Company Resources
- Company devices, email, and internet must be used primarily for official work.
- Excessive personal use during working hours is not allowed.

7. Disciplinary Action
Violation of this Code may result in warnings, suspension, or termination of employment depending on the severity.
