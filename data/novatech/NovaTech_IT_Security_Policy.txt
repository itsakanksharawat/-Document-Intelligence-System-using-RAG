NovaTech Solutions - IT and Data Security Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose
This policy ensures the protection of company data, systems, and devices.

2. Password Policy
- Passwords must be at least 10 characters long and include numbers and special characters.
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Sharing passwords is strictly prohibited.

3. Device Security
- Company laptops must have screen lock enabled.
- Employees must not install unauthorized software.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 2 hours.

4. Data Protection
- Confidential data must not be stored on personal devices.
- Official documents should only be shared through approved company tools.

5. Internet and Email Usage
- Visiting unsafe or inappropriate websites is not allowed.
- Official email should not be used for personal communication.

6. Reporting Incidents
Any security incident or suspicious activity must be immediately reported to itsecurity@novatech.com.
